Your privacy

Last updated 14 November 2026

The short version: everything you write in Ever Faithful is saved only on your phone. We can't see it. There's no account, and we never collect your name, email address or phone number.

What stays on your phone

Your manna and stones (the words, titles, dates, scriptures, topics and favourites) and your settings (appearance, reminder choice, the name you add to a book cover, and your PIN, stored in scrambled form) are saved in your browser's storage on your phone. They are never sent to us or to anyone else.

What we receive, and why

  • Visit counts. Once a day, when Ever Faithful is opened, we count the visit and note whether it was opened from the home screen or a browser. This tells us how many people use the app. The counting service uses no cookies, doesn't follow you across other websites and never receives anything you write. Like any website, it receives basic technical details such as your browser type when the count is sent. We only ever see totals.
  • Notifications, only if you turn them on. Your phone gives our reminder service an anonymous push address (a web address created by your phone's notification service, not your name or number). We store it with the schedule you chose (daily, weekly or monthly, the day and the time) and your time zone, so each reminder arrives at the right moment. Reminders never include anything you wrote.
  • Messages you send us. If you email us, we receive your email address and your message, and use them only to reply.

Why we're allowed to use it

Notifications and messages rely on your consent: you choose to turn notifications on or to write to us, and you can withdraw that at any time by turning notifications off. Visit counts rely on our legitimate interest in knowing how many people use Ever Faithful, done in a way that can't identify you.

We don't sell your information, show you ads, or use your information for automated decisions or profiling.

Who helps us run Ever Faithful

We use a small number of service providers, and none of them receive anything you write in Ever Faithful:

  • a web hosting provider, which delivers the app to your phone. Like any web host, it processes technical details such as your internet address when the app loads;
  • a privacy-friendly analytics provider, for the visit counts above;
  • a cloud provider, which runs our reminder service and stores the notification details above;
  • your phone's notification service (Google on most Android phones, Apple on iPhone), which delivers each notification to your phone;
  • an email provider, for messages you send us.

The app's fonts are stored with the app, so no font service sees your visit. After your first visit, Ever Faithful also works without internet.

Voice typing

When you tap the microphone, your phone's or browser's built-in speech service turns your words into text. On many phones (for example, Android phones using Chrome) this service is provided by Google, and your voice may be processed on their servers under their privacy policy. On iPhone, Apple provides it. Ever Faithful never receives or stores recordings of your voice. If you prefer, you can type instead.

Where your information is handled

Our service providers may handle the small amount of information described above on servers outside Nigeria, including in the United States and Europe.

How long we keep it

  • Your entries: on your phone until you delete them. We hold none.
  • Visit counts: kept as totals for as long as Ever Faithful runs. They can't identify you.
  • Notification details: until you turn notifications off. If your phone stops accepting our notifications (for example, because you deleted the app), we delete them automatically.
  • Emails you send us: only as long as we need to help you, and no longer than 12 months.
  • Hosting records: our web hosting and cloud providers keep technical records for a limited time under their own policies.

Backups, books and share images

These files are made on your phone and only go where you send them.

Books and share images are made to be read, so anyone you give them to can read them.

Your backup file holds all your entries. It's scrambled so it can't be read at a glance, but it isn't locked with a password, so only send it to yourself.

What the PIN does and doesn't do

The PIN lock hides Ever Faithful from people who pick up your phone. Your entries are not encrypted, so someone with technical skills and access to your unlocked phone could still read them. If you forget your PIN, it can't be recovered by anyone, including us. You can reset the app and restore your entries from your backup.

Keeping or deleting your entries

  • Delete any entry from inside the app.
  • Turn off notifications from Menu, then Reminders, and your notification details are deleted.
  • Deleting the app, clearing your browser's site data or losing your phone erases your entries. Back up first if you want to keep them.

Your rights

Under Nigeria's Data Protection Act 2023, and similar laws in Canada, the United States and elsewhere, you have rights over your personal information, including the right to be informed, to access it, to correct or delete it, to object, and to withdraw consent. Because your entries stay on your phone, you already control them fully, and a backup gives you a copy of everything. For anything we hold, such as notification details or an email you sent us, contact us and we'll help.

You also have the right to complain to the Nigeria Data Protection Commission (ndpc.gov.ng). If you live outside Nigeria, you can complain to your local data protection authority, such as the Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada.

Children

Ever Faithful doesn't ask for anyone's personal details. If you're under 18, please ask a parent or guardian before turning on notifications.

Changes to this page

If anything here changes in a way that affects you, we'll update this page and tell you inside the app before the change takes effect.
