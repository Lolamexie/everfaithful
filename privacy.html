Your privacy

Last updated 11 November 2026

The short version: everything you write in Ever Faithful is saved only on your phone. We can't see it. There's no account, and we never collect your name, email address or phone number. If you turn on notifications, our reminder service keeps only what it needs to send them, explained below.

What is saved, and where

Your manna and stones (the words, titles, dates, scriptures and tags) and your settings (reminder choice, appearance, and the name you add to a book cover) are saved in your browser's storage on this phone. They are not sent to us or to anyone else.

If you turn on the PIN lock, your PIN is stored on your phone in scrambled form, not as the numbers you chose.

What we can see

Only a count of how many times Ever Faithful is opened each day, and whether it was opened from the home screen or a browser. If you turn on notifications, we also hold your reminder schedule, as explained under Reminders. We use GoatCounter, a privacy-friendly counting service that uses no cookies and doesn't follow you across other websites. It never receives anything you write. Like any website, it receives basic technical details when the count is sent, such as your browser type, and we only ever see totals.

Voice typing

When you tap the microphone, your phone's or browser's built-in speech service turns your words into text. On many phones (for example, Android phones using Chrome) this service is provided by Google, and your voice may be processed on their servers under their privacy policy. On iPhone, Apple provides it. Ever Faithful never stores recordings of your voice. If you prefer, you can type instead.

Hosting

Ever Faithful is hosted on GitHub Pages, a service of GitHub, Inc. Like any web host, GitHub's servers process technical information such as your internet address when your phone loads the app, so they can deliver it and keep it secure. The app's fonts are stored with the app, so no font service sees your visit. After your first visit, Ever Faithful also works without internet. If you turn on notifications, Cloudflare's servers process the same kind of technical information when your phone talks to our reminder service.

Reminders

You choose how Ever Faithful reminds you, and you can change or stop it at any time from the menu.

Notifications. If you turn them on, your phone gives our reminder service a push address. It is an anonymous web address created by your phone's push service (Google on most Android phones, Apple on iPhone), not your name or number. We store that address with the schedule you chose (daily, weekly or monthly, the day and the time) and your time zone, so the reminder arrives at the right moment in your day. We don't store your name, phone number, email address or anything you write in Ever Faithful, and the reminders themselves say nothing about your entries.

The reminder service runs on Cloudflare Workers, a service of Cloudflare, Inc. Your phone's push service (Google or Apple) delivers each notification to your phone. When you turn notifications off in Ever Faithful, we delete your push address and schedule. If your phone stops accepting our notifications (for example, because you deleted the app), we delete them automatically.

Calendar. If you choose your calendar instead, the reminder is added to your own phone's calendar and we receive nothing. If you choose Google Calendar, the reminder is saved in your Google account like any other event.

Backups, books and share images

These files are made on your phone and only go where you send them. Anyone you send them to can read them. Your backup file holds all your entries, so only send it to yourself.

What the PIN does and doesn't do

The PIN lock hides Ever Faithful from people who pick up your phone. Your entries are not encrypted, so someone with technical skills and access to your unlocked phone could still read them. If you forget your PIN, it can't be recovered by anyone, including us. You can reset the app and restore your entries from your backup.

Keeping or deleting your entries

  • Delete any entry from inside the app.
  • Deleting the app, clearing your browser's site data or losing your phone erases your entries. Back up first if you want to keep them.
  • Because we hold none of your entries, there's nothing for us to delete or hand over.

Your rights

Data protection laws, including Nigeria's Data Protection Act 2023 and the laws of Canada and the United States, give you rights over your personal information. Ever Faithful doesn't collect or store your personal information, so we have nothing to access, correct or delete on your behalf. If you have a question, please contact us.

Changes to this page

If anything here ever changes, we'll update this page and tell you inside the app before the change takes effect.
